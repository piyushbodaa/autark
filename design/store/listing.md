## Full description (4,000 characters max)
Autark is an AI assistant that lives on your phone. Draft a message, brainstorm ideas or learn something new, even in airplane mode. Nothing you type ever leaves your phone.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no sign-up, no ads and no tracking.
• Your chats are stored only on your phone and are never backed up to the cloud.
• Autark has no servers. The internet is used once, to download the AI model.

WORKS OFFLINE
After a one-time 1.3 GB download, Autark works anywhere: on a flight, in the metro, or with no signal at all.

SIMPLE
One tap downloads the model, and the chat opens as soon as it's done. There's nothing to set up.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Write emails, messages and wishes
• Get topics explained simply, for you or your kids
• Brainstorm names, ideas and plans
• Chat in English, and in many other languages too

MAKE IT YOURS
• Instructions that Autark follows in every chat
• Creativity and reply length
• "Think before answering", for maths and tricky questions
• Short, medium or long chat memory
• Delete all chats in one tap

UNDER THE HOOD
Autark runs Qwen 3.5 2B (Apache 2.0) with llama.cpp (MIT), tuned for phone processors.

GOOD TO KNOW
• Needs about 1.3 GB of free space.
• Runs best on recent phones with 6 GB of RAM or more.
• Like any AI, Autark can make mistakes. Check important facts, and don't rely on it for medical, legal or financial advice.
• See a bad reply? Tap Report under it. This opens your email app with the reply filled in; nothing is sent unless you send it.
